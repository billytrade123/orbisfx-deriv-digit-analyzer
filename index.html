Demo testing checklist

1. Let the app collect at least 100 ticks before reviewing statistics.
2. Record a hypothetical Matches or Differs choice before the next tick; don't change it after seeing the result.
3. Compare outcomes over a large sample and include the actual payout/price proposal before judging profitability.
4. Keep real-money trading disabled while testing. This app never places trades.

Important

Matches wins only when the final contract tick ends in your selected digit. Differs wins when it ends in any other digit. A higher raw hit rate does not automatically mean a profitable contract: payouts, costs, sample size, and actual digit distribution matter. The default signal is WAIT because frequency alone is not a validated predictive edge.
